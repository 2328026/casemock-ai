# CaseMock AI — 2-Minute Video Demo Script

Follow this script to record a concise, high-impact demo video for your submission.

---

### [0:00 - 0:25] Hook & Problem Statement
* **Visual:** Screen shows CaseMock AI landing page (`http://localhost:3000`).
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Hi everyone! This is CaseMock AI, built for the Product Intern Assignment. When preparing for Product Management interviews, text chatbots like ChatGPT fail because live interviews are 80% about vocal pacing, communication structure under pressure, and reading visual cues. Peer mocks are difficult to schedule, so we built Alex: an interactive AI Avatar PM Interviewer."*

### [0:25 - 1:15] Live Interactive Sparring Walkthrough
* **Visual:** Click "Product Design & Sense" track $\to$ Select "Design an ATM for children aged 6 to 12" $\to$ Click "Start Live Mock Interview".
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Let's select the Product Design track. Alex greets us out loud and presents the case question. Notice Alex's reactive emotional states—as I click 'Tap to Speak' and talk, Alex listens, nods, and maintains eye contact. I speak my 3-step CIRCLES structure out loud, and Alex dynamically evaluates my framing, takes notes, and asks a sharp follow-up on user segmentation and trade-offs."*

### [1:15 - 1:45] Instant Diagnostic Scorecard & Telemetry
* **Visual:** Complete Turn 3 $\to$ Diagnostic Scorecard modal pops up.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"At the end of the session, Alex generates an instant PM Competency Diagnostic. We see scores across Structure, User Empathy, Metrics, and Vocal Delivery, along with our percentile rank and actionable improvement tips. We can also see our measured confidence gain."*

### [1:45 - 2:00] Live Validation Hub & Next Steps
* **Visual:** Click "Live Validation Hub" to show session telemetry and recent user feedback $\to$ Click "Slide Deck" to show presentation.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"CaseMock AI includes a built-in Telemetry Hub tracking real user sessions, NPS ratings, and waitlist leads for specialized Google and Meta tracks. Thank you!"*
