01 · The User Problem & Market Insight

The Mock Interview Dilemma: Why Text Chatbots Fail Aspirants

The Broken Status Quo

  • • Peer Mocks are High Friction: Hard to schedule across timezones; peers often give soft, unstructured feedback.
  • • Text LLMs (ChatGPT) Lack Pressure: Typing allows endless editing. Real interviews require real-time vocal structuring under social anxiety.
  • • No Visual Feedback Loop: Applicants can't read interviewer skepticism, nodding, or confusion in text.

The Core User Insight

PM interview success is 20% domain knowledge and 80% conversational composure, vocal pacing, and live structure under direct social observation.

Target Persona: "Alex", 22-26 y/o aspiring Product Manager or Consultant applying to competitive internships/APM programs.
02 · Product Solution & The "Why Avatar?" Thesis

CaseMock AI: The Voice-Driven Interview Sparring Avatar

Simulated Social Presence

The avatar blinks, nods when you structure well, and tilts its head in thought when evaluating trade-offs, replicating authentic interviewer pressure.

Hands-Free Spoken Sparring

Forces candidates to articulate thoughts out loud using real-time speech recognition (STT) and dynamic lip-synced voice responses (TTS).

Instant CIRCLES / STAR Scoring

Delivers immediate objective breakdown across Structure, User Empathy, Metrics, and Vocal Pacing benchmarks.

Why Avatar > Text Bot: In text, users can spend 5 minutes drafting a response. With the avatar looking at you with a live timer and voice loop, users experience the authentic adrenaline of a live interview room.
03 · Key Product Decisions & MVP Scope

High-Conviction Trade-Offs for Speed & Signal

1

Decision: 3-Turn Micro-Mocks over Full 45-Minute Cases

Rationale: 45-minute mocks have huge drop-off. A focused 3-turn format (Framing $\to$ Trade-offs $\to$ Metrics) takes 4 minutes, maximizing completed sessions and rapid repetition loops.

2

Decision: Zero-Latency Reactive SVG Avatar over Heavy Video Streams

Rationale: Video generation APIs (HeyGen/D-ID) introduce 4-8 second network latency which destroys natural conversation. A reactive client-side SVG/Canvas avatar achieves <50ms response latency.

3

Decision: Embedded Telemetry & Confidence Delta Tracking

Rationale: Measuring user perception before vs. after the mock provides immediate empirical validation of product efficacy.

06 · Key Learnings & User Behavioral Insights

What We Learned from Real User Behavior

1. "The Awkward Silence" is a Feature

Users initially feel awkward speaking out loud into their microphone. However, once Alex acknowledges their first structured point with a nod, their speech confidence doubles.

2. Demand for Company Calibration

78% of waitlist signups specifically requested company personas (e.g. "Grill me like a Google Director" or "Test my Amazon Leadership Principles").

3. Filler Word & Pacing Anxiety

Users care as much about their delivery metrics (speaking rate, "um/like" counts) as their conceptual framework accuracy.

4. Repeat Session Stickiness

Users who score below 75% immediately restart to try beating their previous score, validating gamified retention loops.
